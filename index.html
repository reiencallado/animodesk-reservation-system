Frequently Asked Questions

Can I reserve a seat for a future date?

Yes. Choose a lab, date, and time slot, then confirm your available workstation seats.

How can a technician help?

Technicians can make reservations on behalf of students and manage lab bookings from a dedicated panel.

What happens to my password?

Passwords are encrypted before storage, and the system validates credentials securely on every login.

Where are policies located?

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use links appear in every page footer for quick access.
