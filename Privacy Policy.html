Privacy Policy

AnimoDesk is committed to protecting your privacy and securing your account information. This page explains how we collect, use, and store data within the system.

Information We Collect

  • User account details like name, email, role, and student ID.
  • Reservation records including lab, date, time slot, and seat selections.
  • Profile preferences used to improve your booking experience.

How We Use Data

  • To authenticate users securely and preserve session state.
  • To show available lab reservations and manage booking workflows.
  • To provide profile management and account-specific history.

Security

Password information is hashed before storage and session cookies remain active only during the browser session. We do not share your personal details with external parties.
